Samiel

The AI systems studio I'm currently building, not yet a legally incorporated company, but the umbrella that Monowire, Morphix, and Orizonz all sit under.

Samiel is the parent concept behind everything else here: what it means for an AI to have a persistent, addressable presence somewhere real, not just a chat window. It's an open-ended thesis rather than a finished product, and it's deliberately being built in the open, one research thread and one shipped system at a time.

Each project under Samiel investigates a different piece of that thesis. Monowire looks at continuity of reasoning between a human and their AI-assisted work. Morphix looks at how organizational knowledge should be structured before it's ever handed to an AI. Orizonz looks at reasoning about what's likely to happen next in a business. None of them are finished; all of them are documented as they go.
