Orizonz

A Samiel research project reasoning about what's likely to happen next in a business, tracing relationships across markets, technology, and behavior that are hard to see from any single signal.

Most forecasting looks at one signal at a time: a market trend, a technology shift, a behavioral pattern in isolation. Orizonz starts from a different premise, that what actually moves a business rarely announces itself in a single data source. It's the interaction between markets, technology, and behavior, the kind of relationship that's invisible until you're specifically looking for the connection between them, that produces the signal worth acting on.

This is early-stage, open-ended research rather than a finished system. Like everything under Samiel, it's being documented as it develops rather than announced once it's complete.
