Monowire

A continuity layer for AI-assisted work, built to preserve the reasoning behind a project so it can be picked back up after time away without reconstructing it from scratch.

AI executes work. Humans provide direction. Monowire exists in between: it stores the project's evolving reasoning, not just its tasks or commits. Git records what changed in the code. Monowire records why decisions were made and what should happen next, so a human returning to a project after hours, days, or months can rebuild their mental model almost instantly instead of reconstructing it from scratch.

Underneath a single, calm conversation, Monowire runs a hidden organization: a Mission Controller that coordinates department specialists, and a deterministic Mission Kernel that owns organizational state, since AI should never be the sole source of that truth. The user experiences one thoughtful conversation. The company behind it plans, documents, and prepares for execution the entire time.
